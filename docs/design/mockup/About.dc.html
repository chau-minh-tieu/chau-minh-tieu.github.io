About

I’ve been building software since 2010, starting with data and reporting, then moving into software engineering and leadership across Vietnam and Singapore. Today I’m an engineering manager, responsible for people and technical direction.

I came to machine learning through quantitative research, building models and data pipelines for financial markets, a field that punishes anything that only works on paper. I currently focus on engineering leadership, system design, cloud, machine learning and AI.

I’m also interested in startups and solo founding, especially how far one engineer can go with AI. I’m building Reign Edge, an AI-native trading platform, as a side project.

When I’m away from the keyboard, I travel, usually toward the sea or up a mountain trail.

This site is where I write down what I learn, so I understand it properly.

How posts are written

Every post starts as a brief I write myself: the argument, the reader, and what I actually learned. A model drafts from that brief. I review every line in a pull request, check the sources, and cut what I would not say. If a post gets something wrong, that is my mistake, not the model’s.
